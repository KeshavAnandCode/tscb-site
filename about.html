about us

drive, commitment, and passion every day

TSCB is a vibrant community of cricket-loving students dedicated to promoting the sport. Our mission is to share, grow, and engrain cricket into the American sports culture. Founded in December of 2025, TSCB aims to grow into the largest hub for high school cricket in the nation.

As a registered 501c3 nonprofit, TSCB differs from other youth leagues by promoting cricket over profit. TSCB connects with existing cricket club insfrastructures in high schools, aiming to create a network of high schools (similar to football) in the US.

our core values

Community, Service, and Leadership

To create a self-sustained community of cricketers led by high-schoolers.

TSCB exists to provide a free and universal platform to spread high-school cricket. While leagues are private and commercialized, TSCB aims to offer a high-school sport-like experience through free games and a central state tournament.

Yes, yes, and also yes: TSCB covers everythign through our connections and sponsors.

Contact us, and we will place you into a league as soon as possible. Right now, our leagues are only in Austin and Dallas, so school outside those cities will have to provide their own transportation to grounds.

TSCB is not recreational: it is a competitive league that is played using tapeball (for preliminary matches) to be more mindful of cricket's steep learning curve (avoiding beginner injuries). Performant teams will advance to the state competition (with cash prizes), our partnership with NCCA aims to bring such competitions in the spotlight of selectors at the collegiant and higher levels.
