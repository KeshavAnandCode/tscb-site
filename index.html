Who We Are

Statewide Cricket Community Led By Students

As the first and only student-led high school cricket federation in the state, we provide a platform where students do more than play: They lead.

From team operations and league coordination to media, officiating, and on-field governance, students are placed at the center of the ecosystem, gaining real leadership experience while shaping the future of high school cricket

our mission

Our Mission to Serve, Connect, and Grow

Our mission is to connect high schools across regions, support the growth of new programs, and work with partner organizations that believe in educational sport.

In connecting the future cricketers of America, TSCB aims to establishing a statewide framework that legitimizes high school cricket, empowers students as leaders, and creates a clear pathway beyond high school participation.
